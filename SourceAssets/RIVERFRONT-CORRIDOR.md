# Two-bank reference corridor

The playable riverfront now runs from game X = -820 m to +840 m on both
banks. The local origin is beside Atal. North is +X and west is +Y. The modeled canopy, railings and deck run 285 m between the two promenade abutments. The longitudinal spacing was checked against the published
coordinate points for the [Sardar](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q29707714),
[Atal](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q113334583) and
[Ellis](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Q1744317) crossings (CC0 Wikidata
coordinate statements). About 590 m south of Atal is Sardar, and about 640 m
north is Ellis. The [SRFDCL Atal page](https://www.sabarmatiriverfront.com/atal-bridge.html)
confirms their ordering. Bank offsets, cross-street layouts and landmark
meshes are original game interpretations; the corridor is straightened and
not a survey or replica of Ahmedabad's city blocks.

[SRFDCL Parks](https://www.sabarmatiriverfront.com/parks.html),
[Event Centre](https://www.sabarmatiriverfront.com/event-centre.html), and
[General Facilities](https://www.sabarmatiriverfront.com/general-facilities.html)
guided the park and market form. [AMC's Ellis Bridge record](https://heritage.ahmedabadcity.gov.in/details/90/heritage_site/en)
describes concrete road spans added beside the earlier steel bridge; the game
retains the heritage deck and its two flanking road spans. The Sardar and Ellis road decks rise on both approaches. A standing character can walk below all four bank portals and reach the stairs beyond the bridges. The native district review records the walked distance and continuous ground contact for each approach.
