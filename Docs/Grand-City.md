# Grand City

Grand City is an offline, third-person city game for Apple Silicon. The first release target is a dense, fictional district measuring **1 km × 1 km**. The M2 MacBook Air is the performance reference. The first playable loop is to leave home, walk through a market, talk to a resident, visit a shop, drive across the canal, and return home. Daylight, evening and rain need to work on that same route.

## What to take from GTA VI

Rockstar’s official presentation shows distinct places within Leonida, including a dense city, the Keys and the Grassrivers, and describes people with connections to homes, businesses and neighborhoods. The trailers use street-level framing, dense storefronts, traffic, groups of people and varied light to establish a place. Grand City can use those design principles in an original setting. The behaviors and budgets below are Grand City design decisions. [Rockstar’s Leonida showcase](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/only-in-leonida) · [Official GTA VI trailers](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI)

| What makes the reference work | Grand City implementation | What to verify in play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each district has a reason to exist | Market and apartment streets west of the canal, public park and newer blocks east of it, with waterside walks and a service area around the north crossing | At street level, the player can identify where they are through the buildings, signs, sounds and street use |
| People appear to be going somewhere | Residents visit shops, wait at crossings, queue, sit, and walk alone or with companions. Nearby people look, yield, greet or make room. Families slow down and regroup at kerbs | Follow a resident for a minute. Their route and activity change for observable reasons, and they can continue after an interruption |
| Cars have weight and presence | Real-scale original unbadged hatchback, sedan, crossover and utility vehicle. Stable fictional plate numbers, interior, steering and wheel travel, and distinct handling | Watch a car park, enter it, drive both bridges, brake, reverse, and check the same car from exterior and cockpit |
| Character motion follows contact | Motion Matching for turns, starts and stops, feet that stay on steps, and separate low vault, fence hurdle and high mantle. Door, seat, bed and garment actions have timed contact poses | Inspect knee flexion, foot slip, hand position, collision recovery and gait transitions in captured clips and live play |
| The city changes through the day | Shop opening, a bus stop queue, evening practicals, quieter residential streets at night, rain affecting ambience and road appearance | Replay the same route in day, night and rain. Keep route signs, people and vehicle silhouettes legible |
| Information follows the action | Existing focused interaction system and native HUD, plus a continuous map, waypoints and a persistent home marker. Vehicle instruments appear when driving | A nearby car and shop can compete without the interact key choosing a different target from the prompt |

Use original names, layout, likenesses, signage, vehicles, music and narrative. Track the source and redistribution terms for every mesh, scan, font, texture, recording and animation supplied to the public project. Assets restricted to local authoring stay outside the public checkout.

Grand City's canal district starts at sunset. Warm sunlight, blue slate shadows and a rose sky bring out the material textures. Coral, sea-green and blue enamel signs mark the market, park and quay. Their emissive strips use the existing material and bloom passes. Daylight remains available for judging materials and legibility in neutral light.

## Stability and the Mac build

The two November 27 editor-game sessions exited during play. Both restored the checkpoint. The game and macOS logs record a process exit, and the available evidence contains no fatal stack for either session. The first playthrough loaded a fitted mesh that exceeded Unreal’s estimated budget for an individual skinned-asset compile. Treat editor asset compilation as a testable risk and keep cause, workaround and reproduction separate in the test record.

1. Run an arm64 cooked Development build from macOS Launch Services for player testing. Cook platform assets in advance with Unreal Automation Tool. Use the editor-game with equivalent settings for a control run. Keep isolated copies of saves, settings and logs in each test directory. [Epic’s cook and package workflow](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/packaging-your-project) · [Epic’s build operations guide](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/build-operations-cooking-packaging-deploying-and-running-projects-in-unreal-engine)
2. Check cold launch, first Continue, first movement from the checkpoint, pause and resume, and repeated open and close of the shop and wardrobe. Repeat with both avatar presets and an older profile with purchased clothes. On the Mac desktop, verify app switching, pointer recapture and reconnecting input devices. Record exit status for supervised runs. Capture Unreal and macOS diagnostics if they fail and retain the executable build, symbols and settings.
3. Trace asset loads, shader creation, render submission and allocations where a reproducible frame or exit identifies a problem. Use Unreal Insights for game, render and asset-load scope, Metal HUD for drawable size and present times, and Instruments Game Performance for CPU/GPU overlap, physical memory and thermal behavior. [Unreal Insights](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/unreal-insights-in-unreal-engine) · [Apple’s Metal HUD metrics](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/understanding-metal-performance-hud-metrics) · [Apple’s Game Performance instruments](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/xcode/analyzing-the-performance-of-your-metal-app)
4. Record Metal's actual shader model and cache status with the benchmark. A cache experiment needs to improve time from Continue to control and the first route together. Compare the cold journey, then the same warm route. A missing cache or precache miss has a different remedy from an expensive steady-state render pass. [Epic’s PSO precaching guide](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/pso-precaching-for-unreal-engine)
5. Keep a single canonical app install and the existing offline profile and Unreal project identity. Give the new map its own checkpoint district. On an invalid checkpoint, restore the player to a validated walkable position with the same wallet, wardrobe and purchases. Test interrupted saves and loading a copied older profile before promotion.

### Air verification on November 27

The signed cooked build loaded a copy of the existing profile with the fitted sweater and ran for 110 seconds at 1328 × 747 and 100% render scale. It exited on the engine test timer. macOS reported a 2.65 GiB peak physical footprint. The profile file matched its original checksum.

The first daylight layout passed 4,216 road-surface probes across both bridges, a walk down the market pavement, and keyboard entry and exit in the parked hatchback. A 120-second offscreen run reached a 2.19 GiB peak footprint. Frame time was 16.60 ms median and 98.10 ms at the 95th percentile. A separate 30 fps capped CSV capture recorded 104.33 ms p95 frame time, 4.76 ms p95 game thread time, 32.87 ms p95 GPU time and 95.36 ms p95 render-thread critical-path idle. File Provider load varied during the capture. These runs identify frame pacing and waiting as the next profiling focus.

The updated art build passed the walk, parked entry and exit, and bridge probes at sunset, daylight and night. The 30 fps capped, offscreen checks at 1328 × 747 and 100% render scale recorded p95 frame times of 43.68 ms at sunset, 35.76 ms in daylight and 40.37 ms at night. Each sample followed the same checks and camera setup. Background load varied and the night sample was shorter. A quiet, windowed playthrough with sustained driving is the remaining performance acceptance test.

The older riverfront driving trial still stalls near a turning vehicle at the bridge in the cooked automated route. Record the junction and steering trace before changing vehicle or collision fidelity.

## Performance targets on the M2 Air

These are acceptance targets. Use the same build type, frame cap, controls, weather, route, antialiasing, drawable dimensions and render fraction for a comparison. Note whether the Mac is plugged in and whether other workloads were active. Record wall time through Continue, first traversal stutter, and sustained performance separately. [Apple’s graphics settings and measurement guidance](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/Metal/improving-your-games-graphics-performance-and-settings)

| Metric | Initial goal for the play preset |
| --- | --- |
| Output | 1328 × 747 window when it fits, default 30 fps target and independent visual preset for higher-end Macs |
| Sustained frames | 20 minutes including a foot route, a full driving loop, day and rain. Aim for p95 ≤ 36 ms and p99 ≤ 50 ms after warmup. Count frames over 50 and 100 ms and retain the worst capture |
| Cold play | Three cold launches and three warm launches, at least one older profile and one fresh profile. Aim for no play-input pause over 100 ms after control is handed over, and regress neither the startup-to-control time nor first-route p95 |
| Memory and thermals | Initial cooked process physical footprint ceiling of 8 GB on the 16 GB Air. Record peak and final footprint, GPU allocation and thermal state. Repeated interior entry and wardrobe changes should plateau |
| Reliability | Zero unexpected exits, hung transitions, missing collision, unreadable or reverted saves in the above acceptance runs. Exercise Quit Game, reopen, Return Home and a full offline relaunch |
| Quality parity | Each optional effect has an A/B trace and close, middle and skyline capture. Start with legacy raster materials, instancing, bounded local lighting and conventional LODs. Evaluate additional features against the installed engine, Metal profile and sustained budget |

The 4K window remains an explicit output option. Measure its real drawable size and render fraction independently. Epic’s Mac feature matrix distinguishes software Lumen, hardware-dependent features and experimental or beta support. The Air preset should succeed with the project’s established raster path. [Epic’s macOS requirements and feature matrix](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/macos-development-requirements-for-unreal-engine)

## Map and systems milestones

1. **Street and bridge layout.** Replace the survey-based layout with an original bounded network. Define durable IDs and authored points for home, market, two canal crossings, park, fuel, parking and service access. One street graph supplies the roadway, road joins, NPC routes and map positions. Probe the four wheel contact paths across ramp starts, deck transitions and tile boundaries. Walk both banks, service lanes and building perimeters. Mark playable interiors distinctly on the map.
2. **First finished route.** Author one continuous home–market–crossing–park loop in the Unreal Editor. Include a furnished home and fitting room, visible shop depth, parking and a functional crossing. Place façades around fire/service access, building footprints and pedestrian doors. Give asphalt a believable aggregate scale, curb cuts, drain grates, expansion seams, repairs, lane paint and wetness localized to drainage. Model bridge parapets, abutments, ramps and deck markings to follow the same drivable surface. Layer warm material and wear variation over a shared, instanced kit.
3. **Cooked world and streaming.** Author reusable street and façade kits with ISM/HISM and Level Instances. Put the final map in World Partition and use editor-generated PCG for reusable dressing, HLOD for distant blocks, and Data Layers for interiors and district content. Size cells from the measured footprint and maximum driving speed. Stream ahead of a moving vehicle and validate the collision cell before a teleport. Use tiered material complexity, texture mips and distance shadow budgets. Profile midday vistas, rain and interior doorways before adding blocks. [World Partition](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/world-partition-in-unreal-engine) · [PCG and World Partition](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/using-pcg-with-world-partition-in-unreal-engine) · [UE City Sample](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/city-sample-project-unreal-engine-demonstration)
4. **Playable people.** Author two fictional adult hero presets with anatomically grounded face, torso and joint proportions, scan-quality skin materials, fitted garment layers, and hair cards for the Air. Use MetaHuman LODs and the Game Animation Sample, then validate IK retargeting and two body types against the existing apartment-to-street motion checks. For clothing, validate skin, base layers and outer layers in order, with separate remove, hold and put-on actions in the mirror and fitting room. Keep five to ten meaningful choices per garment type within the shared skeleton and material budget. Test contact at the bed, wardrobe, kerb, fence and car door before increasing simulation cost. [Game Animation Sample](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/game-animation-sample-project-in-unreal-engine) · [Motion Matching](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/motion-matching-in-unreal-engine)
5. **Street life and vehicles.** Build authored destinations with Unreal navigation and StateTree for near pedestrian tasks. Start with local residents and fictional visitors, paired and family routes, a vendor, and a crossing wait/yield interaction. Budget ambient animals as short, occasional behaviors in parks and waterside areas. Simulate distant crowds with simpler animation and updates, then evaluate MassEntity when near-actor cost is measured. Use Chaos Vehicles for the driven fleet and cheap traffic simulation away from player interaction. Validate grip, wheel contact, collision recovery, vehicle exit clearance, and indoor load before increasing variety. [UE MassEntity overview](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/overview-of-mass-entity-in-unreal-engine) · [Chaos Vehicle setup](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/how-to-set-up-vehicles-in-unreal-engine)
6. **Sound, state and final art.** Add localized footstep surfaces, layered engines, directional traffic, interiors, water and time-of-day ambience. Carry the same interaction, map and wardrobe language into phone, shops and settings. Review windows, awnings and street furniture at human height, inside and outside in rain and night. Validate streaming boundaries, profile recovery and sustained frame times along the entire map after each content pass.

Each milestone needs comparable windowed captures, a playable route, and measured stability and frame timing before increasing world size or density.
